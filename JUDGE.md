# LIMBO, for judges

Live page: https://limbo.edycu.dev/judge (this file is its mirror; both, and the page's live instrument, are generated by `node kit/scripts/judge-build.mjs` from `evidence/`).

> When the connection dies, your app keeps working. When it returns, an item deleted while it was edited comes back as a ghost twin, and a lost claim is shown to its owner.

LIMBO is a local-first skeleton where every op declares how it merges. Kiroween 2026, **Skeleton Crew**: one skeleton (`skeleton/`) and two apps in two folders (`apps/basecamp/`, `apps/aftershock/`). No install, no login. Video: https://youtu.be/V4m3zTV5MPs (2:47). Devpost: https://devpost.com/software/limbo-offline-merge-skeleton-two-apps

## I. Skeleton Crew

- `skeleton/`: `core/` (ops and the 6 named merge rules: `commutes`, `causal-lww`, `resurrect`, `claim-one`, `sum-split`, `ordered-move`), `sync/` (replica, links, the local op log), `limbo/` (fog, twins, the Inspector, FIXED). 21 files and 2,189 lines by the budget count (budget 30 files, 2,400 lines), 15 exported values (`evidence/lean.json`).
- `apps/basecamp/`: a group trip list, 12 ops. Live: https://basecamp.limbo.edycu.dev/?replicas=2
- `apps/aftershock/`: a field survey form on a sector grid, 6 ops, demo data. Live: https://aftershock.limbo.edycu.dev/?replicas=2
- Each app supplies five things: a model, ops, a seed script, views and a config ([docs/ADOPT.md](docs/ADOPT.md)). AFTERSHOCK's 27 Kiro spec sessions changed 0 files under `skeleton/` (later UI polish touched skeleton CSS and markup for both apps).

## II. The 60-second path

1. Open https://basecamp.limbo.edycu.dev/?replicas=2. You should see two cards, **REPLICA A — Sari** and **REPLICA B — Budi**, joined by a cord captioned "SIMULATED LINK — DevTools offline does not cut it; use the lever".
2. Press **Cut link**, then **Play the lake scene**. You should see both cards fog over, each chip reading "Offline — 2 waiting".
3. Press **Restore link**. You should see the tent back as a twin on both cards ("TWIN · Budi deleted this while Sari ticked it · rule: resurrect") and, on Budi's card, "TWIN · Budi and Sari both claimed this · rule: claim-one" with **OK** and **Why Sari?**.
4. Tap **Keep** on the tent twin and **OK** on Budi's lost claim. You should see the **FIXED** stamp on both replicas.
5. Your own edit: type an item into *Add an item* on A, **Cut link**, tick it on A, delete it on B (**⋯** then **Delete**), **Restore link**. It comes back as a twin on both cards (rule: resurrect); **Keep** settles it.
6. Open the **Inspector**, then press **Run shuffle test**: "1,000 / 1,000 orderings → same state" (your browser replays the ops on screen; not the 10,000-ordering run).
7. Open https://aftershock.limbo.edycu.dev/?replicas=2: **Cut link**, **Play the survey scene**, **Restore link**. Sector C3 reads UNSAFE, "5 reported — Sari 2, Budi 3"; **Keep Sari's note** or **Keep Budi's note** lands FIXED.

## III. Run it on this page

The live page embeds two BASECAMP replicas running the skeleton's `createPair` and BASECAMP's ops (`site/src/judge.ts`, bundled to `site/assets/judge.js`, loaded on first hover, focus or press). A checklist ticks from the engine's own values, and the shuffle replays the ops on screen in 1,000 random causal orders. What the same code printed when the page was built:

- **After Cut link + the lake scene:** chips “Offline — 2 waiting” and “Offline — 2 waiting”; 2 + 2 ops in limbo; fingerprints `88b3705c` ≠ `99a93050`
- **After Restore link:** chips “Live · 2 conflicts” on both; the tent is a twin on both replicas; Stove (the last one) → Sari on both, Budi sees the twin; fingerprints `7e11f8e1` = `7e11f8e1`
- **After Keep + OK:** FIXED on both replicas; fingerprints `88b3705c` = `88b3705c`
- **Shuffle:** 1,000 orderings of 21 ops → 1 final state, `88b3705c`, the state both fixed replicas hold

## X. Honest limits

1. **Convergence is measured, not proven.** 10,000 random orderings of 3 replicas with property tests is strong evidence, not a proof.
2. **There is no cross-device relay.** The live apps sync two replicas through an in-page link labelled as simulated. DevTools "offline" does not cut it; the dev-only relay in the e2e suite ships in no production build.
3. **Offline claims are provisional.** No CRDT can stop two offline people both claiming the last stove. On reconnect the declared rule picks one claimant on every replica and the other is told.
4. **The kit showed no measurable effect.** The pre-registered A/B pilot: 0 of 10 vs 0 of 10 held-out failures, p = 1. Its 8 kit-arm Stop blocks were false alarms from the kit's own vacuity guard, and 2 of 10 kit-arm runs edited that guard; the fix (the guard is now hash-protected) has not been re-piloted.
5. **Not every product commit is Kiro’s.** 9 of 124 product commits were made outside Kiro (listed with their subjects in share.json), and the ledger holds 17 hand rows in the ledger (16 edits made outside Kiro, 1 attribution correction), each with its reason.
6. **The Stop gate blocked twice in 194 build sessions.** Once unplanned, once on a fault we seeded to prove it fires. It allows at most 3 blocks a session, and it skipped 19 times before the property harness existed.
7. **Kiro CLI only.** No logged session used the Kiro IDE, its Spec Correctness (property-based) or the Agent Hooks panel, or Powers. The MCP server was used lightly: 8 calls from 1 session.
8. **The skeleton is over its first budget.** 21 files and 2,189 lines by the budget count: inside the restated 2,400-line budget, over the original 1,800.
9. **Demo data, no outside users.** AFTERSHOCK runs on data we wrote and no field use is claimed; we have no outside-user data for either app.
10. **Only the apps work offline.** The landing page and this page are static pages with no service worker.
