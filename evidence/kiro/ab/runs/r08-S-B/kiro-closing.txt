  - State keeps every report keyed by op id, so `apply` is idempotent and doesn't mutate its input.
  - The shown severity is the highest severity among reports that no other report has seen (the causal frontier).

  - idempotence and no mutation of the input
- `sector.report.props.ts` and `sector.report.props.test.ts` run the kit's `max` intent check. Every report also counts as a "clear" for the reports it has seen, which is how lowering gets tested.
