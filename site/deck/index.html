The fixer

Every op declares its merge.

  1. causal-lwwTicks, namesThe write that saw the other wins; concurrent ones twin.
  2. commutesAdds, sharesBoth edits count.
  3. claim-oneClaimsOne winner; the other sees a twin.
  4. resurrectDelete vs editIt returns as a ghost twin.
  5. ordered-moveReorderOne stable order.
LIMBO
Each op must pass convergence and intent properties before the agent may finish, and no rule reads a wall clock: order comes from causal context.

The honest part

Two claims. One winner, named.

No CRDT can stop two offline people claiming the last stove. Claims stay provisional; on reconnect every replica picks the same winner, and the loser is told.
