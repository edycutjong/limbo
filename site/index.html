Plate III · Merge rules

Every op says how it merges before it ships.

Each op must pass convergence and intent properties before the agent may finish: every replica ends on the same state, and on the one its rule declares. No rule reads a wall clock: order comes from causal context.

resurrect

Deleted while edited elsewhere: it comes back.

The deleted item returns as a ghost twin beside the edited one. One tap keeps or drops it, and every replica holds the same list afterwards.

claim-one

Two offline claims: one winner, named.

No CRDT can stop two offline people from claiming the last stove. Claims stay provisional; on reconnect every replica picks the same winner (lower Lamport stamp, then replica id), and the loser sees a twin naming them.

Property: after convergence every replica names the same winners, never more than the item has units.

causal-lww

Ticks, names, notes

The write that had seen the other wins. Concurrent edits that differ come back as a twin. A clock never decides.

commutes

Adds and shares

Both edits count. Severity takes the worse; expense shares sum to the total in whole cents.

ordered-move

Reorder

Two concurrent moves land in one stable order. The losing move is not shown as a twin.

Kiro wrote most of the product. The hooks kept the ledger.

Questions a judge asks first

Is the demo on this page real?

Yes. It bundles BASECAMP's ops and the LIMBO skeleton unchanged (site/src/hero.ts). The link between the two replicas is simulated, because DevTools "offline" does not cut it.

Is it really offline?

Each app is a PWA: after one visit it reopens in airplane mode, with its edits kept in IndexedDB. Live end-to-end tests against both deployed apps passed 6 of 6, offline reload included. Not built: a relay between two devices; the replicas sync inside one page.

Can two offline people both claim the last stove?

Both claims are provisional. On reconnect, claim-one keeps exactly one claimant on every replica and shows the other person a twin that names the winner.

Did Kiro really write the code?

115 of 124 commits in skeleton/ and apps/ carry a Kiro-Session trailer that maps to a row in evidence/kiro/sessions.jsonl. The other 9 are listed in share.json.

Does the kit make Kiro better?

Our pre-registered pilot found no measurable effect (0 of 10 vs 0 of 10). What it did show was a gap in the gate, which we then closed (the fix has not been re-piloted).

Can I build my own app on the skeleton?

Supply a model, ops with declared merges, a seed, views and a config. docs/ADOPT.md walks through it; the property suite runs over every registered op.

Cut the link yourself. Watch the tent come back.
