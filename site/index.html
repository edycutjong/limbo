LIMBO

For people whose shared list or form breaks when the signal does, and for builders who need offline merges that do not diverge.

When the connection dies, your app crosses over, keeps working on the other side, and comes back without losing an edit. LIMBO is a small local-first skeleton in which every op declares its merge rule, plus a /.kiro kit that makes Kiro good at the merge logic that looks right and diverges when operations arrive in a different order.

Two apps, one skeleton
